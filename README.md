## Four Problems Solved in One Native Mac App

1. **Whole-Home Music Across Every Brand:** Stream **Spotify, Apple Music, YouTube, web browsers, DJ apps, or local lossless FLAC/ALAC** across **AirPlay 2 speakers & TVs**, **Google Cast & Nest speakers**, **DLNA Smart TVs** (lossless PCM), **`/app` browsers**, and **This Mac** together—with live album art, smart `Commercial Break` cards, TV remote controls, and zero virtual audio drivers.
2. **Multi-TV Live Window & Google Cast Video Sync (`v2.3.0`):** Click **Cast Window…** to sync **unprotected browser tabs, Keynote decks, or local video apps** at `1080p30 H.264` (`1080p15 Eco` and `4K30` 1 click away) from a **single `1×` Apple Silicon hardware encode** (`~1–3%` total system CPU at `1080p30`; Intel Macs & `4K30` vary; `0%` when idle) across **unlimited Google Cast / Chromecast / Google TV / Android TV / Sony Bravia TVs**, **unlimited DLNA Smart TVs**, **unlimited `/app` browsers**, **1 AirPlay TV Video** (macOS OS limit), and **This Mac** simultaneously—while audio-only Nest speakers stay locked on lossless audio. *(Honest DRM & browser note: built strictly on Apple's official `ScreenCaptureKit`; macOS automatically protects FairPlay/HDCP services such as Netflix, Disney+, Apple TV+, and YouTube TV/Movies, and Safari's hardware video layer can black out single-window capture on some streaming sites—for standard YouTube or Twitch, use Chrome/Firefox or Display capture.)*
3. **Automatic Zero-Echo Sync (`0–10,000 ms`):** Tap **Auto-Sync (Tones)** and your Mac's microphone array measures acoustic lag across AirPlay 2, Apple TV, Google Cast TVs/speakers, Smart TVs, and This Mac strictly in memory, locks every room to the exact same beat, and **shuts the microphone off automatically**—backed by optional **Dual-Stage Watermark Lock** and thumb-friendly `±50 ms` room nudges in `/app`.
4. **Instant Local 4K HDR10/HLG, `.mkv` & 5.1 Movie Studio (`v2.3.0`):** Share Mac or external USB SSD folders—or **drag and drop any `.mkv`, `.mp4`, `.mov`, or `.m4v`** onto CastBar's window or Dock icon (*Open With → CastBar*, `LSHandlerRank = Alternate`, never hijacking your defaults)—to play immediately inside CastBar's **built-in zero-encode Mac video player** with native fullscreen, `.srt`/embedded subtitles, and **USB-SSD-safe cross-screen Continue Watching** (resuming across Mac, AirPlay TV Video, Google Cast TVs, `/app`, and Multi-Room Sync).
   - **Verified audio & HDR:** Direct Play (`~0.1%` CPU) streams untouched 4K HDR10/HLG; pure-Swift `~0.2 s` container-only remuxing (`~0.5%` CPU) preserves HDR10/HLG color metadata and passes **5.1 `AC-3` / `E-AC-3` surround** & `AAC` untouched to Apple TV 4K, AirPlay TV Video, Safari & Mac, auto-converts FLAC/ALAC/Opus/MP3 to 320 kbps AAC, selects companion compatible tracks (or Direct Play) for DTS/TrueHD, and serves universal stereo 320 kbps AAC + WebVTT subtitles to Google Cast TVs. *(Note: `.webm` streams via Direct Play to compatible players/browsers.)*

## Up and Running in 30 Seconds

1. **Open CastBar on Your Mac** — **Mac Audio** and **Cast Window…** work immediately. Drop any `.mkv`/`.mp4`/`.mov` onto CastBar or its Dock icon to play right away, or click **+ Add** to share Mac or USB SSD folders.
2. **Pick Any Mix of TVs & Speakers** — **1-click from Mac:** open **Devices** to pick **AirPlay 2** speakers, **1 Apple TV / AirPlay TV**, **unlimited Google Cast TVs & Nest speakers**, and **This Mac**. **Smart TVs & browsers:** open **CastBar** in your TV's built-in Media Player (DLNA) or scan the TV QR code for `/app`.
3. **Sync & Press Play** — Start **Spotify**, **Apple Music**, **YouTube**, or a 4K movie, and tap **Auto-Sync (Tones)** to lock every room to the exact same beat—your Mac mic turns off automatically, and you can nudge any room `±50 ms` from `/app` on your iPhone.

## Verified macOS System Requirements

- **macOS 12 Monterey or later (Apple Silicon & Intel Universal):** Local library & USB SSD streaming, built-in zero-encode 4K HDR10/HLG `.mkv` player, Drag-and-Drop (*Open With → CastBar*), Continue Watching, AirPlay 2, AirPlay TV Video, Multi-TV Google Cast Video & Audio, DLNA Smart TVs, `/app` Cinema Web Player + optional Web Access Lock, and 3-Mic Auto-Sync.
- **macOS 13 Ventura or later:** Adds **Mac System Audio capture** (Spotify, Apple Music, web browsers) AND **Live Mac Window Sync** (`ScreenCaptureKit`).
- **macOS 14.2 Sonoma or later (Recommended):** Unlocks native **Core Audio Process Taps** (zero-echo simultaneous This Mac + TV audio, *App Audio Only* isolation, and no macOS 15 Sequoia recurring Screen Recording prompts for audio-only streaming) plus native `SCContentSharingPicker`.
